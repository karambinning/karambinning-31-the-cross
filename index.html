A Note From The Cross

Quirky, retro-brilliant, art-deco-tastic — and unmistakably Worcester.

Step inside and you'll find brass, deep wood and tiled floors lit like a slow weekend morning. We pour for the cyclists in cycling kit, the laptop-and-flat-white crowd, the parents in for pancakes, the early-evening cocktail set, and absolutely everyone in between. Everyone is welcome.

What we do

A café, a kitchen, an afternoon-tea room and a small evening venue — all under one roof on The Cross.

№ 01

The Café

Brooklyn-inspired brunches, sourdough toasts, eggs every way, and the kind of flat white you actually want to sit with. Counter service, table service, no fuss.

№ 02

Afternoon Tea

Served every day — loose-leaf tea, a tiered stand, and a quiet corner. Booking advised on weekends.

№ 03

Cocktails & Evenings

House cocktails with a speakeasy flair, sharing plates, and a short, considered wine list. Live music on selected nights — follow on Instagram for what's on.

№ 04

Private Hire

Two floors available for private parties, work events, baby showers, birthdays. Drop us a line and we'll talk through the room.

№ 05

Delivery & Pickup

Skip the queue at peak hours — order coffee, brunch and lunch for pickup, or get it delivered locally in Worcester.

№ 06

The 31 Club

A quiet newsletter — new menus, seasonal specials, live music announcements. No noise, just the worth-knowing.

Find Us

On the corner, where it's always been.

We're at 31 The Cross — the Georgian building on Worcester's pedestrianised high street, a short walk from the Cathedral and the river. Walk-ins welcome; booking advised for weekend brunch, afternoon tea and evening tables.

For private hire, large groups or any event enquiry — call us, or message on Instagram.
